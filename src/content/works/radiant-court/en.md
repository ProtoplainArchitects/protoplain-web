街屋各向的日光。洸庭

Upon  entering, daylight spills through the atrium,\
while  the aroma of cooking drifts upstairs to greet the children.\
The  living and dining rooms flow seamlessly together, destined to become the  heart of family memories.\
A  gentle breeze weaves through every corner, completing the imagined beauty of  a home for four.\
The  design preserves a sense of privacy and independence,\
while  ensuring that each space feels connected, never apart.\
Here,  sound, light, fragrance, breeze, and warmth intertwine—\
together  shaping the most radiant scene of home.\
Home  is not merely a unit of measurement.\
It  is a long-held wish finally realized,\
  a singular, one-of-a-kind space.
