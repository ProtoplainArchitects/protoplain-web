subtitle: "The Sound of Flowing Water in the Valley"

山谷的溪流聲。竹泊

From  the slightly raised entrance, one steps into an enclosed circular space that  opens toward a broad platform for rest and reflection.\
Beneath  the bamboo canopy, one can gaze into the distance, listen to nature’s  symphony, breathe deeply, meditate, or stretch, immersed in the sheer force  of a waterfall cascading head-on.\
A  wide-span bamboo structure captures the fivefold sensory experience of  Basianshan.\
The  form is defined by a 12-meter arched canopy supported at two points, its  perimeter framed in steel tubing and interwoven with 8 cm vertical Moso  bamboo members in a cross-braced lattice to uphold the sweeping roof. The  load is distributed through the bamboo lattice and steel pipes to the steel  beams on either side, further dispersing the force to three steel columns per  side, which connect to newly reinforced beam foundations. These in turn  integrate seamlessly with the existing platform, preventing an over-concentration  of structural stress and ensuring stability.
