都市山腳的夕陽。大明文創會館

The  site is located on the edge of Taichung City, at the threshold between the  urban fabric and the shallow mountain landscape. It combines the  accessibility of the city with the potential of nature. For students arriving  from across the region, this is not only a convenient place to learn, but  also fertile ground for artistic creation and ecological experience, serving  as an educationally significant extension of the campus.\
At  the neighborhood scale, the site sits beside the Tanzi District Office and  the busy Fengxing Road. Amid the density of the city’s fabric and rhythm of  daily life, the design inquiry emerged: how might we create an artistic  sanctuary that belongs equally to students, the community, and education. Our  design takes “wind, light, and culture in coexistence” as its central theme.  The intent is to extend the vocabulary of the existing campus, embody the  educational spirit of the Ta-Ming brand, and skillfully draw upon the site’s  topography and natural conditions to shape a cultural center that unites  function with poetry.\
The  architecture is composed of geometric “box” volumes, offset, interlocked, and  stacked to form a clean, modern façade language. Service boxes are enclosed  with solid walls, giving a sense of stability, while exhibition spaces open  toward the city with semi-transparent polycarbonate panels, creating a light,  luminous expression—an architectural metaphor for enlightenment and openness  in education. The voids between volumes are infilled with large expanses of  glass, generating transparency and fluidity, so that wind, light, and people  flow naturally through the building.\
Inside,  varied ceiling heights enrich the spatial layering of the galleries and allow  flexible exhibition settings. From the street-facing culinary teaching area,  circulation guides visitors deeper into the interior exhibition spaces,  linking multiple points of learning and cultural engagement. Interspersed  atriums bring in daylight and cross-ventilation, aligning the building with  natural rhythms and infusing its spaces with vitality.\
Where  wind slips through,\
the  architecture learns to breathe.\
Light  touches walls, steps, and eyes,\
not  only illuminating, but sparking thought.\
The  space does not shout;\
it  speaks softly:\
art  belongs to the everyday,\
education  can also be poetic.\
Boxes  of light rise as a stage for learning,\
where  nature and culture converge.\
Here,\
wind  becomes the guide,\
  light the language,\
and  people the story of the space itself.
