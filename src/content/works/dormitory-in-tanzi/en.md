美家移工宿舍

Thisis a place where people living far from home can find both life and growth.\
Conceivedaround a new model of dormitory living, the project integrates shared spacesfor education and daily life while prioritizing comfort through naturalventilation and daylighting to adapt to seasonal and diurnal changes.\
The vision was to soften the distance of 3,000 kilometers with a spacethat gathers memories of home, creating a sanctuary where energy can berestored. Here, the dormitory becomes more than a shelter—it becomes a truehome, where residents find belonging in one another.
