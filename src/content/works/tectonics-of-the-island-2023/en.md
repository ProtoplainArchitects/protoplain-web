第三屆「島嶼構竹」新銳建築展

*Tectonics  of the Island*  originates from a series of bamboo architecture movements launched in 2021,  aimed at showcasing the ecological, cultural, and industrial vitality of  Taiwan. Building upon the structural systems and joinery explorations of the  first two *Bamboo New Field* exhibitions, this third edition presents  seven unique works, each reinterpreting and integrating bamboo into everyday  life—rekindling trust in bamboo as a building material.\
The  bamboo movement is a contemporary continuum. Beyond advancing technical  research and material development, Tectonics of the Island reflects on the  choices of materials and construction in architecture today, and on how  bamboo might reclaim its place in the modern architectural landscape. With  its cultural depth, local industrial potential, and remarkable capacity for  carbon sequestration, bamboo not only contributes meaningfully to the global  pursuit of net zero, but also embodies a mutually beneficial relationship  between people and the environment.\
Centuries  ago, bamboo was a cornerstone of building in Taiwan, before receding with the  arrival of modern industry, leaving a gap in construction knowledge. Through  this exhibition, we collaborate with emerging architects to research, innovate,  and reimagine every stage of design and building practice—rediscovering  bamboo, our long-familiar companion, in a new light.
