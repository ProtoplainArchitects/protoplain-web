---
title: "Zhan House in Shigang"
subtitle: "Fields of Wind and Light"
---

田野的風與光。石岡詹宅

Amid the  orchards of Shigang, a 200m2 residence emerges from the careful  transformation of an ancestral home. It is both a three-generation dwelling  and a retreat for a brother returning from abroad, embodying the dual role of  home as the heart of daily life and a vessel of memory and emotion.\
The design  begins with respect for nature, attuning to the paths of wind and light so  that the architecture arises in harmony with its surroundings. The sloped  roof and bird-steps speak not only in form but as a dialogue with the past,  while the orchard and trees frame a central courtyard that extends the  lineage of traditional farmhouses toward the present and future.\
At the  threshold between the natural and the constructed, the project explores the  instinct of materials and the potential of architecture. Bamboo, timber,  concrete, roof tiles, and the orchard environment interweave, revealing  overlooked yet ever-present rhythms—a larger order that grounds the home.\
Ultimately,  the house becomes an inclusive domain: one that shelters the daily lives of  elderly farmers while embracing the rhythms of a new generation; one that  bridges memory and renewal, transforming the wind and light of the fields  into the enduring landscape of home.
