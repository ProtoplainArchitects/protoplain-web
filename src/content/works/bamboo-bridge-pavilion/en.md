Mist of the Mid-Mountain Forests
##
中海拔山區的霧。竹虹

At  the corner of the Yunling Hill parking lot, this bamboo installation creates  a natural rest stop and viewpoint for travelers. Conceived as a rainbow-like  arch, its large-span structure demonstrates how bamboo’s innate properties  can be transformed into architectural expression. When visitors arrive at  Yunling Hill, they encounter a plaza designed as both a gathering point and a  place of pause. The space also establishes a symbolic gateway, where natural  tectonic art conveys the sense of entry into the mountain landscape.

- Structural Design：A.S studio Engineering Consultant
