鑛客 | 第一屆礦山藝術季

The  inaugural Mine Arts Festival in 2020 was held in Jinguashi’s Qitang Old  Street. After five months of curation, selection, participation, and  creation, six works were chosen. ProtoPlain’s The Miner was born out of  everyday village life, the nostalgia of a mountain town’s past, and the  surrounding natural landscape.\
An  abandoned hydraulic structure, overgrown with grasses and blooming  wildflowers, mirrors the silvergrass swaying across the slopes—its dampness  recalling the lingering songs once carried through the mine shafts.\
Through  the gathering of residents and the pauses of visitors, fragments of flavor  and memory drift in the air, drawing neighbors closer, offering a taste of  the town’s quietude, and reviving a gilded chapter once sealed away.\
Beside  Qitang Old Street, in a house quietly reclaimed by greenery, mirrored steel  poles stand as sentinels. They chime softly with wind and rain, scattering  halos of mist and refracted light in irregular patterns—an echo of the  fragmented memories embedded in the tunnels. The unchanging rhythm of fog,  rain, and silvergrass has sung for a century the ballads of the mining years.  Visitors to Guashan are invited to linger, to listen, to wait for the mist  and the rain—to sit, share a drink with the mountain town, and uncover its  stories in person.
