溪石上的葉舟。浮竹軒

Set  at a key junction where the riverside recreation area, Basianshan Villas,  restaurants, and trails converge, the Toilet of Bamboo Rising is the largest  restroom facility at the park entrance.\
Adjacent  to the expansive Soapberry Stone Meadow, with its gentle slope and the murmur  of flowing water, doubles as a waiting and resting spot, while also serving  as a venue for orientation or educational events.\
Beyond  rebuilding the restroom itself, through thoughtful circulation planning,  curated landscaping, and the use of natural materials, we aim to create a  restroom space that seamlessly integrates functionality, education, and  healing.\
Above,  a bamboo roof floats like a leaf in midair.\
Composed  entirely of bamboo, the roof employs five arched trusses in sequence,  projecting outward to form a welcoming semi-outdoor area that embodies  structural stability and bamboo’s aesthetic grace.\
On  both sides, hinged roof panels lift to enhance daylight and ventilation,\
overturning  the common perception of public restrooms as dark, damp, and unpleasant.  Instead, the facility is transformed through a harmonious blend of local  character, natural materials, and architectural aesthetics.\
Situated  beside the steep drop of the Shiwen River, the project incorporates micro-hydropower  to supply renewable energy to the restroom’s electrical system.\
In  the façade, 20 cm reinforced concrete inner walls pair with 15 cm rammed  earth outer walls to create a composite structure. The rammed earth’s  stratified textures are prominently expressed on the façade, evoking the  layered passage of time within the forest and embodying the enduring presence  of stone and earth.
