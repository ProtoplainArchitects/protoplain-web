身心信任的空間。豐原葉宅

“Ahome is where the body and mind find rest. Take care of your true self, andeverything else will fall into place.”\
Constrainedby the typical conditions of a collective housing unit, Ye House inevitablyfaced the necessity of choice. We chose to seek breadth within limitation,compacting the arrangement of bedrooms to return greater generosity to theshared spaces. The design defines volumes as “element-clusters” within theplan, each cluster of elements responding to daily life while preservingspatial flow for light and wind to move freely through every corner.\
Theseelement-clusters function as multi-layered cabinets and furnishings—storage,display, and partitions—scaled to different daily routines and needs.\
Adwelling, we believe, should be a space attuned to delicate perception.Materiality, therefore, was chosen with a commitment to authenticity: linen,solid wood flooring, birch plywood, laminated timber, bamboo composites,mineral paints, etc. Each was considered for its tactile grain and naturalbehavior, forging a dialogue between substance and inhabitant.\
The result is a home that speaks in whispers, placing itself gently yetfirmly at the heart of daily life, holding the subtle yet profound weight ofliving.
