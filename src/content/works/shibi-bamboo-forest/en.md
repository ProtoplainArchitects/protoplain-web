石壁竹創森 二期計畫

As Taiwan’s first forest-therapy-focused eco-tourism site centered on bamboo, this nearly 100-hectare sanctuary invites visitors to reconnect with nature through bamboo architecture and immersive landscape design. With ecological restoration, upgraded trail systems, and carefully curated aesthetics, the forest comes alive once more, offering a renewed connection between people and the natural environment.\
The project is fully aligned with the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), aiming to create a healing space where body and mind can be cleansed and the air flows fresh and free. Moving forward, the initiative continues to bring together government agencies, local communities, tourism partners, and schools to promote a design-driven travel experience. Through these efforts, travelers from Taiwan and around the world can experience a place where environment, design, and spirit engage in meaningful dialogue.\
The Shibi Bamboo Forest has been thoughtfully structured to accommodate various modes of visitor engagement through three experiential zones: the General Zone, the In-depth Experience Zone, and the Quiet Zone. The General Zone centers on the stretch from the Wooden Horse Trail to Yunling Hill, featuring landmarks such as Stillness Seats, Circular Bamboo Pavilion, Bamboo Bridge Pavilion, and Seats of Ridge. The In-depth and Quiet Zones offer deeper, more secluded experiences, with highlights like Whisper Lounge, Breeze Pavilion, Echo Dome, and Courtyard Restroom. Adventurous visitors can ascend to the Breeze Platform for sweeping mountain views, while those seeking a gentler pace can follow a loop trail through the bamboo groves—designed for rest, reflection, and renewal. Every path invites connection, offering something for every traveler, regardless of pace or preference.

With minimal intervention and profound respect for nature, the design team restored neglected bamboo groves into a renewed landscape, enhancing local ecology and creating an ideal setting for forest therapy. Located at mid-to-high elevation, the trails offer fresh mountain air and abundant negative ions, helping visitors find calm and renewal in nature’s embrace.\
Beyond restoring the ecosystem, the project enhances community life through education and leisure, improving overall quality of living. To maintain the landscape’s purity, artificial elements like solar panels were avoided. Instead, the design embraces ecological harmony and sustainability—environmental, social, and economic—fostering a healthier future for generations to come.
