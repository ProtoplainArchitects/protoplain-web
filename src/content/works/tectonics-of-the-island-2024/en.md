第四屆「島嶼構竹」新銳建築展

Exhibition  sites are located at Jhihben National Forest Recreation Area, the lakeside  area of Taitung Forest Park, and the Taromak Indigenous community. Six  participating teams—Eric Chen, Mu-Yi Ko, Szu-Yin Chen, Lawrence Chien, Pe-Jen  Wang, and Ahung Masikadd—each capable of both construction and volunteer  engagement, embraced the challenge of creating bamboo architecture that  resonates with the shifting landscapes of Taiwan’s east coast.\
Guided  by the Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency, Ministry of Agriculture (MoA)  and organized by the Taiwan Bamboo Society, the *Tectonics of The Island*  exhibition enters its fourth edition in 2025, moving from the west coast to Taitung—hoping  to see contemporary bamboo architecture flourish across the island. While the  first two editions focused on structural breakthroughs and joinery  techniques, and the third sought to integrate bamboo more closely into  everyday life, this edition continues to cultivate bamboo architecture with  an insular character. By responding to the pulse of our time and reflecting  on the relationship between people and the environment, it moves a step  further toward defining “authentic architecture.”
