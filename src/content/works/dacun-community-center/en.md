林媽廟共生共榮的社區場域。大村活動中心

"*Honor  the elderly in your family and extend the same care to the elderly of others;  cherish the young in your family and extend the same care to the young of  others.*"\
  The Dacun Community Center embodies this spirit in built form. Located on a  site shared with Linma Temple and the original community center—which for  years also housed the township’s kindergarten—the project brings together  faith, education, and community life. To better serve the elderly, Linma  Temple was relocated from the third floor to the ground level, reflecting the  warmth of local relationships and the ethic of mutual care that defines the  community.\
Through  multi-functional spatial planning, the project strengthens neighborhood bonds  while cultivating a culture of art, exercise, recreation, and lifelong  learning The community center is not only a place for gatherings and rest; it  also hosts elder care visits, senior meal programs, and community college  classes, becoming a new hub for cultural engagement and social exchange.\
In  terms of planning, the design retains the original private passageway and  archway, preserving the collective memory of the lane. Both the existing  community center and Linma Temple are kept in place, with new corridors  linking old and new structures to support a continuous flow of activities. On  the ground floor, a flexible multi-purpose lobby connects directly to the  community plaza, accommodating a wide range of events. North-facing windows  invite soft daylight, while planting and louvers on the south side filter  harsh sunlight. The double-layered roof with an air cavity effectively  buffers heat and rain, creating pleasant spaces for both indoor and outdoor  activities. Semi-outdoor zones further extend opportunities for residents to  gather, socialize, and rest. The architectural language blends local building  traditions with contemporary expression. Red brick, lattice screens, and  pitched roofs are reinterpreted to form a modern structure rooted in Dacun’s  cultural identity.\
The  shouts of basketball games in the temple courtyard,\
the  clicks of chess pieces under the shelter,\
the  sizzle and fragrance rising from the community kitchen.\
Incense  drifting from Linma Temple mingles with blossoms in the plaza,\
scents  and memories lingering with the village.\
Diverse  spaces, each with their own circulation,\
allow  residents to move freely—like neighbors dropping by each other’s homes.\
Linked  with the old community center and temple courtyard, this is not only an  addition of space,\
but  an expansion of community ties and shared emotions.\
Where  there is human affection, there is mutual reliance.\
Where  culture takes root in people’s hearts,\
community  thrives.\
The  Dacun Community Center, embraced by Linma Temple, is a place of coexistence  and shared prosperity.
