伸港周宅

The site is located in Shengang Township, a typical long and narrow plot of land found in urban and rural areas. The residents are four close-knit sibling families. Although each family has its own household, they maintain frequent gatherings and interactions. Therefore, striking a balance between "individual independence" and "family sharing" became the core design challenge.

The design breaks away from the traditional horizontal, multi-level layout of townhouses, incorporating staggered floor slabs to establish vertical spatial connections. The gaps created by the staggered floor slabs not only create visual permeability but also effectively introduce natural light and ventilation, improving the common problems of poor lighting and stagnant air in street-front houses. A core space comprised of stairs and a courtyard connects the living room, dining room, family room, and reading nook, allowing daily activities to flow through the three-dimensional space, adding layers and interactivity to life.

To cope with the strong winter winds of Shengang's coastal location, the public and gathering spaces are located on the south-facing side, combining the front yard, balcony, and shared corridor to form a continuous semi-outdoor living area, encouraging natural interaction and visits among family members. Furthermore, by borrowing scenery, the design incorporates views of the surrounding greenery and park, allowing the interior to reflect the changing light and shadows and the flow of breezes throughout the seasons.

This project attempts to reinterpret the possibilities of townhouses, establishing a new rhythm of life between shared and private spaces, enabling family bonds to continue to flourish within the space, and shaping a warm and layered residential landscape.
